# Problem Statement & Project Scope

## Problem Statement
Many university students struggle with managing daily nutrition, understanding body mass index (BMI) requirements, and tracking intake against energy expenditure. Most commercial calorie tracking tools are complex, require paid subscriptions, or lack localized diet recommendations suitable for student life.

## Scope of the Project
The **Student Diet & Calorie Log** system provides a lightweight, interactive solution that:
1. Calculates a student's BMI and standard target intake based on body metrics.
2. Offers localized, category-specific food suggestions.
3. Allows real-time tracking of food consumption and workout activity.
4. Generates summary logs for daily progress tracking.

## Target Users
- University and college students looking for quick diet tracking.
- Health-conscious individuals seeking calorie targets without complex setups.

## High-Level Features
- Dynamic BMI and Daily Energy Target computation.
- Pre-populated local food nutrition database with manual fallback options.
- Real-time intake and burn adjustments.
- Input error sanitization and file-based logging.
